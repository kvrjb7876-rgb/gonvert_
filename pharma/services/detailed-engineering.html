Engineering & Design

Knowledge-Based Engineering for Quality by Design

All Services

Our knowledge-based approach identifies risks early and embeds compliance into every design decision.

Quality cannot be tested at the end. It must be designed in from the start.

At Gonvert, we apply a knowledge-based engineering approach, grounded in Good Engineering Practice (GEP), to deliver facilities that are compliant, robust, and inspection-ready.

We begin by understanding your process, identifying critical risks, and making science-based design decisions. The result is fewer deviations, faster qualification and validation, and lower lifecycle costs. Rather than correcting issues during validation, we prevent them during engineering.

As your EPCm partner, we document every critical decision, creating a clear and traceable path to compliance. Your facility is not only built—it is designed, documented, and proven.

Key Engineering Services You Will Benefit From at Gonvert:

  • Knowledge-Based Process & P&ID Design — aligned with cGMP requirements, manufacturing flow, and risk assessment
  • GEP-Compliant Cleanroom & HVAC Engineering — supporting contamination control strategies
  • Clean Utility Engineering — WFI, Purified Water, Clean Steam, CIP/SIP, and Process Gases
  • Black Utility Engineering — Chillers, Boilers, Cooling Towers, Compressed Air, and Vacuum Systems
  • Electrical Engineering & Power Distribution — MV/LV systems, UPS, Generators, and Area Classification
  • Critical System Identification — focusing design effort on systems that impact product quality and compliance
  • 3D Modeling & Clash Detection — reducing construction risks and rework
  • Lifecycle Engineering Approach — from design through commissioning and qualification
  • Documented Engineering Decisions — ensuring traceability and supporting regulatory inspections
  • Value Engineering — optimizing cost without compromising quality or compliance
