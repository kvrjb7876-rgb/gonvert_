Conceptual & Basic Design

Feasibility studies, facility concepts, and basis-of-design that align capacity, compliance, and capital from the outset.

All Services
Image area — reserved

Every facility begins with a question of fit: does the concept match the capacity, the compliance path, and the capital available. This is the stage where a facility strategy is tested against those three constraints before detailed engineering resources are committed.

Basis-of-design work done here carries forward through the entire delivery lifecycle — the assumptions set at this stage shape everything from cleanroom classification to commissioning scope, so they are set deliberately, not provisionally.
