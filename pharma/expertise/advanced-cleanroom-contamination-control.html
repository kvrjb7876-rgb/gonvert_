Advanced Cleanroom Systems & Contamination Control

From cleanroom architecture to contamination control strategies, we deliver solutions that support product quality, regulatory compliance, and Annex 1 expectations.

All Expertise

Product quality begins with the environment in which it is manufactured. At Gonvert, we design and deliver advanced cleanroom systems that support contamination control, operational efficiency, and regulatory compliance throughout the facility lifecycle.

Through long-standing collaboration with a reputable European cleanroom systems manufacturer, we combine proven technologies with GMP engineering expertise to develop clean manufacturing environments aligned with Annex 1 expectations and modern contamination control principles.

Our expertise extends to Annex 1 compliance, Contamination Control Strategies (CCS), and risk-based facility design. Working closely with Engineering, Quality, Production, and Validation teams, we ensure environmental control remains aligned with operational and regulatory requirements.

The result is a facility that minimizes contamination risks, improves operational performance, and maintains confidence during inspections, audits, and long-term operation.

Key Areas of Cleanroom Expertise:

  • Cleanroom Concept & Facility Planning – aligning environments with process requirements
  • Cleanroom Systems Design & Integration – supporting GMP-compliant operations
  • Cleanroom Fabrication & Installation – delivering proven cleanroom solutions
  • Contamination Control Strategy (CCS) – supporting Annex 1 compliance
  • HVAC & Environmental Control – maintaining critical operating conditions
  • Material & Personnel Flow Design – reducing contamination risks
  • Lifecycle Cleanroom Compliance – supporting long-term operational readiness
