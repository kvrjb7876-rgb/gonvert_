Lifecycle Verification

Reduce qualification effort without compromising compliance. We apply modern lifecycle verification principles that improve efficiency, traceability, and inspection readiness.

All Expertise

Reduce validation effort without sacrificing compliance.

Modern GMP projects require a smarter approach to verification. At Gonvert, we apply lifecycle verification principles based on ASTM E2500 to focus effort on the systems, functions, and parameters that truly impact product quality, patient safety, and regulatory compliance.

By combining Good Engineering Practice (GEP), science-based decision-making, and SME-led risk assessments, we develop verification strategies that reduce unnecessary testing and documentation while maintaining complete traceability and regulatory confidence. Our approach integrates verification activities throughout the project lifecycle rather than treating qualification as a separate activity at the end.

Working closely with Engineering, Quality, Operations, Suppliers, and Validation teams, we help ensure that critical requirements are identified early, verified efficiently, and supported by documented evidence from design through operational readiness.

The result is a streamlined qualification process, faster startup, optimized project resources, and a facility that is inspection-ready, fit for intended use, and prepared for long-term compliant operation.

Key Areas of Lifecycle Verification & ASTM E2500 Expertise:

  • ASTM E2500-Based Verification Strategies – focusing effort on critical quality requirements
  • SME-Led Risk Assessments – identifying systems that impact product quality and patient safety
  • Requirements Traceability Management – linking design, verification, and operational requirements
  • Good Engineering Practice (GEP) Integration – building quality into the project lifecycle
  • Supplier Documentation & Test Leverage – maximizing the value of existing verification evidence
  • Operational Readiness Planning – preparing facilities for efficient startup and handover
  • Inspection Readiness Support – ensuring confidence during audits and regulatory inspections
