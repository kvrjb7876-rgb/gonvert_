Project Highlight

Herbal Extracts & Natural Health Products Manufacturing Facility

DeliveryEPC
Scale5,500 m²
Completed2022

01Scope

EPC delivery of a 5,500 m² GMP-compliant manufacturing facility for herbal extracts and natural health products, including softgel capsules, suppositories, and ointments. The project encompassed extraction, production, warehousing, QC laboratories, packaging operations, clean and black utilities, and fully integrated building and environmental management systems.

02Challenge

The facility was required to accommodate diverse herbal raw materials and multiple dosage forms while maintaining strict control over temperature, humidity, cross-contamination, and product quality. Compliance with international GMP requirements, WHO guidelines, GACP principles for herbal materials, and regulatory expectations for qualification, validation, and inspection readiness was critical.

03Solution

Engineered and constructed a fully integrated GMP facility featuring dedicated herbal extraction areas, softgel manufacturing suites, and semi-solid production lines. The design incorporated steam-based dehumidification systems, automated process controls, centralized BMS and EMS platforms, qualified clean and black utility systems, and comprehensive Commissioning, Qualification, and Validation (CQV) documentation. The facility was engineered to support compliance with EU GMP, WHO GMP, ISO 14644 cleanroom standards, and international regulatory expectations.

04Result

Successfully delivered in 2022, the facility provides a modern, highly automated manufacturing platform for herbal extracts and natural health products. It was delivered with complete CQV and verification documentation, supporting GMP compliance, regulatory readiness, and efficient long-term operation.
