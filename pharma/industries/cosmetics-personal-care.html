Project Highlight

Baby Care Products Manufacturing Facility

DeliveryEPC
Scale5,500 m²
Completed2013

01Scope

EPC delivery of a 5,500 m² manufacturing facility for premium baby care products, building on long-established technical, quality, and manufacturing practices developed during the client’s earlier corporate history. The project included production areas for baby shampoo, diaper rash cream, and baby powder, as well as warehousing, quality control laboratories, utilities, packaging operations, and supporting infrastructure.

02Challenge

Baby care products require exceptional standards for product safety, hygiene, and consistent quality. The facility had to support multiple product formats, including liquids, creams, and powders, while maintaining contamination control, managing allergens, maintaining precise environmental conditions, and complying with cosmetic GMP requirements and international quality standards.

03Solution

Engineered and constructed an integrated manufacturing facility featuring dedicated production suites, controlled material and personnel flows, HVAC and environmental control systems, clean and black utilities, automated process systems, and quality-focused packaging operations. The facility was designed in accordance with GMP principles and international best practices for cosmetic manufacturing to enable efficient, scalable production.

04Result

Completed in 2013, the project provided a modern manufacturing platform for premium baby care products, capable of producing multiple product types while maintaining high standards of quality, safety, and operational efficiency.
