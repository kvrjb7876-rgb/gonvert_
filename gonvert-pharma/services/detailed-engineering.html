Detailed Engineering

Multidiscipline detailed design — architectural, process, mechanical, electrical, and automation — coordinated to one model.

All Services
Image area — reserved

Detailed engineering is where the basis-of-design becomes a coordinated, buildable model. Architectural, process, mechanical, electrical, and automation disciplines are developed together rather than in sequence, so conflicts are resolved on the model, not on site.

Because a pharmaceutical facility's disciplines are tightly interdependent — process drives mechanical, mechanical drives electrical, all of it drives the cleanroom envelope — coordination at this stage is what keeps the design constructible and the schedule realistic.
