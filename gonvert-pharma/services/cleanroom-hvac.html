Cleanroom & HVAC Systems

Classified cleanrooms and HVAC engineered for contamination control, pressure cascade, and ISO-class stability.

All Services
Image area — reserved

The cleanroom envelope is the physical expression of a facility's contamination-control strategy. Classification, pressure cascade, and HVAC design are engineered together so the environment holds its classified state reliably, not just at commissioning but through years of operation.

This work is designed to the classification requirements of ISO 14644, giving the environment a measurable, auditable basis rather than a general assumption of cleanliness.
