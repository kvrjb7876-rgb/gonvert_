GONVERT Pharma
(01) Who We Are

Pharmaceutical facilities demand more than construction — they demand engineered compliance.

GONVERT Pharma Technology delivers specialized EPCm engineering for the pharmaceutical and life-sciences sector. From conceptual design and feasibility through commissioning, qualification, and validation, every decision is measured against regulatory expectation and operational reality.

We engineer controlled environments — cleanrooms, HVAC, clean utilities, and process systems — where contamination control, data integrity, and repeatable performance are designed in from the first line, not corrected at handover.

The result is advanced manufacturing infrastructure that is compliant by design, efficient to operate, and ready to qualify: precise engineering in service of patient safety and product quality.

Precision

Tolerance-driven design and measured execution across every discipline.

Compliance

cGMP and regulatory intent embedded from concept to qualification.

Performance

Facilities designed to run reliably, efficiently, and at scale.
