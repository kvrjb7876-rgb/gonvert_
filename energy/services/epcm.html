EPCM Services

Independent oversight and professional management throughout project execution — safely, efficiently, on schedule, and within budget.

All Services

EPCM is where the plan meets the site. As an independent Engineering, Procurement, and Construction Management partner, GONVERT provides oversight across every discipline without holding a fixed-price EPC position — keeping our recommendations aligned with the client's interest rather than a construction margin.

This is the stage that carries a project from a fully engineered, procured asset through to a commissioned, documented handover.
