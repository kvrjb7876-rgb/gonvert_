Project Development & Investment Advisory

Evaluating opportunities and preparing investment-grade documentation to support sound development and financing decisions.

All Services

We help clients evaluate opportunities, assess project viability, and prepare investment-grade documentation to support sound development and financing decisions. This is the entry point of the GONVERT lifecycle — the stage where an opportunity is tested rigorously before capital or engineering resources are committed.

Work at this stage is technology-neutral and evidence-based: energy yield is modeled to recognized confidence levels, costs are structured for scrutiny by investors and lenders, and the resulting documentation is built to withstand due diligence.

  • Feasibility Studies
  • Project Screening & Opportunity Assessment
  • Financial Modeling
  • CAPEX / OPEX Analysis
  • Energy Yield Assessment (P50/P90)
  • Investment Memorandums
  • Funding Readiness Support
  • Bankability Assessments
