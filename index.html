(01) About Us

At GONVERT, we don’t just execute projects — we engineer trust, performance, and progress.

GONVERT Technology Solutions Co. was formed through the consolidation of two established firms with decades of proven success in Pharmaceutical Engineering and Clean Energy.

By uniting their technical mastery, financial strength, and project leadership, GONVERT delivers excellence at every stage of the EPCm lifecycle.

With deep expertise in engineering, procurement, construction, and high-level consulting, we support clients in making informed, strategic decisions that balance compliance, sustainability, and profitability. We operate with precision and integrity — from conceptual design and feasibility studies to commissioning and operational support.

Driven by innovation and responsibility, GONVERT’s purpose is to build efficient, compliant, and future-ready facilities. Through our projects and partnerships, we create opportunities and deliver measurable value that endures.
